- Every calculated feature point must support a human-readable explanation grounded in applied rules and source evidence ([memo 009, 001.c](docs/memo/memoWX_009_designPatternForChanFeatures.md)).
- Derived series retain one-way references to immutable source series; merged items identify source ranges within that exact series ([memo 009, 001.b](docs/memo/memoWX_009_designPatternForChanFeatures.md)).
- Keep `KlineTSeries` a classic OHLCV series; indicator calculations and results stay outside `Kline` and `KlineTSeries` ([memo 009, 001.a](docs/memo/memoWX_009_designPatternForChanFeatures.md)).
